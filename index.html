CVE-2024-38193 NOV 2024 CRITICAL · 9.8
Windows AFD Driver Race Condition Leading to LPE
A Use-After-Free (UAF) vulnerability exists in the Windows Ancillary Function Driver (afd.sys), triggered by a race condition in the IOCTL dispatch path. Combined with heap spraying, this allows for Local Privilege Escalation to SYSTEM.
